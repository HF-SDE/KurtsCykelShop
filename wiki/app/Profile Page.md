On the profile page, you can view and manage your personal information, settings, and preferences.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HF-SDE/KurtsCykelShop/refs/heads/main/.github/images/wiki/profile.png" height="500">

From the profile page, you can access various features such as:
- Change your password (see instructions below)
- Log out of the app
- View your profile information

# Change Password
To change your password, follow these steps:
1. Tap on the "Change Password" option on the profile page.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HF-SDE/KurtsCykelShop/refs/heads/main/.github/images/wiki/profile%20change%20password%20button.png" height="500">

2. Enter your current password, followed by your new password and confirm the new password.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HF-SDE/KurtsCykelShop/refs/heads/main/.github/images/wiki/profile%20reset%20password%20empty.png" height="500">
3. Tap the "Save" button to update your password. If the password change is successful, you will receive a confirmation message. If there are any issues (e.g., incorrect current password, weak new password), you will see an error message with details on how to resolve it.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HF-SDE/KurtsCykelShop/refs/heads/main/.github/images/wiki/profile%20reset%20password.png" height="500">

# Log Out
To log out of the app, simply tap the "Log Out" button on the profile page. This will end your session and return you to the login screen. Make sure to save any unsaved changes before logging out to avoid losing data.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HF-SDE/KurtsCykelShop/refs/heads/main/.github/images/wiki/profile%20logout.png" height="500">
