When you first open the app you will see a login screen. 
![Login](.github/images/wiki/profile.png)

You can log in with your username and password combination, but for testing purposes you can use the following credentials: 
- Username: `admin`
- Password: `admin`

After entering the credentials, tap the login button. If the login is successful, you will be redirected to the profile page where you can access different features based on your permissions. If you encounter any issues during login, make sure to check your internet connection and verify that the backend server is running properly.

see [Profile Page](profile) for more details on what you can do after logging in.
